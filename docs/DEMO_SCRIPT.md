# Demo script: four minutes in the room

Presenter: Jordache. Matt frames if he is willing; otherwise Devrin. Mock mode on. Wifi off is fine. Signed in as Matt Burke. Start on Portfolio in August (Shift+P, Reset to August, if the screen is not already there).

Click budget: the ERP story with zero clicks (hover), the questions with one, a cited page with two.

## Beat 0: the problem, in his words (20 seconds, Matt or Devrin)
"Every month twelve management teams tell us what they are going to do. Holding them to it depends on remembering what they said in January. I don't." Then: "This is the first time the firm can see, on one screen, whether they did."

## Beat 1: Monday morning (25 seconds)
Portfolio, August. Three companies, twelve initiatives, eight months of cells. Work strip: Need a conversation 2, Slipping 2, Not reported 1, On track 7. "Green means what they said still holds. Amber means a date or a number moved. Red means it moved twice, or by a quarter. Grey means they stopped mentioning it."

Hover the last cell on Harlan's ERP row, the red one. The card: "Phase 2 is now expected in Q1 2027," August report, page 2. "Every cell is a sentence from a report, with the page number. No clicks yet."

## Beat 2: go back in time (35 seconds)
Press **Go back in time** in the header. The page becomes a card; the earlier months stack up behind it. Click **March** on the timeline. The March card slides forward: nine reports read, everything green but one.

Hover the ERP cell in March: "Implementation on track for a June cutover. Data migration is 60% complete." March report, page 2. "In March this was fine. It was fine in April too. Now watch what the tool does when the next report lands."

## Beat 3: watch it read (50 seconds, the wow)
Press **Add April reports** in the bottom bar. The reading log streams: each report, each initiative, the sentence, the change, the flag. April slides forward. Still green.

Press **Add May reports**. Read the ERP line aloud as it appears: "Now targeting a July go-live. Vendor resourcing is constrained in the integration workstream." Date moved: June to July. Slipping. The May card slides forward and the cell is amber. "One month, one sentence, one flag."

Press **End** on the keyboard (or click August on the timeline). The cards slide through June and July to August: the cell is red, the strip reads 2 / 2 / 1 / 7. "Nobody lied. The date moved three times in five months, one month at a time, and each month it read fine on its own." Press **Return to August**.

If the reading log is running long, press **Skip to the end**; the month still arrives.

## Beat 4: before Thursday's call (30 seconds)
Press **Open** on the ERP row. The stack: January "on plan for the end of Q2," March "June cutover," May "targeting a July go-live," June "phase 2 in Q4," August "phase 2 now Q1 2027." Change chips on March, May, June, and August.

Right rail: three drafted questions, each citing the month it came from. Read the first one aloud. Press **Approve for Thursday**. "He goes into the call with these. He didn't spend Sunday night re-reading eight PDFs."

Point at "What the reports do not say": no cost of delay, no revised budget since March. "It flags the gap. It does not fill it."

## Beat 5: the page number is real (15 seconds)
Click the May cite. The May report opens at page 2 with the sentence marked. Back. "Nothing here is the tool's opinion. It is what management wrote, side by side."

## Beat 6: ask it (25 seconds)
Click **Ask the ledger** in the header and pick **What has Corvus stopped reporting?** The answer: the March sentence about the two territory roles, then "Not mentioned in April," May, June, July, August, with "Not mentioned since March" on May. "Answers are quotes with pages. If the reports do not say it, the tool does not say it." Escape.

## Beat 7: across companies (40 seconds, James's lens)
Patterns. Card one: Harlan and Corvus are evaluating the same TMS vendors, Corvus is three months ahead. Card three: two go-live dates slipped citing "vendor resourcing," neither report names the vendor. "This is the part a person cannot do. Reading twenty-four reports at once and noticing two companies used the same phrase."

Card two: press **Add the comp-plan learning to Harlan's Thursday questions**. The draft types out. Press **Approve**. "That is now question four on Harlan's page, with James's June learning behind it. Nothing was sent."

Right rail: the learning log. "James keeps this by hand today, on his own OneDrive, and nobody else can read it. Here it is shared, and each entry is a draft until he confirms it."

## Beat 8: after the call (25 seconds)
Press **Add from a note** (or Shift+P, **Dictate a note**). The transcript plays. A draft log entry and a current-state line appear on Meridian. Press **Review**. "Two minutes of talking after a plant visit, and the record updates. The learning is kept in his format, not the model's."

## Beat 9: the scale picture (15 seconds, Devrin)
Shift+P, jump to `monday`. Twelve companies. "Three companies today. The portfolio is twelve, and the record starts on day one of the next deal. Every initiative the firm underwrites, tracked from the memo to the exit."

## Q&A
"How does it get the reports": "Today a person drops the monthly pack in a folder. In production it watches the SharePoint folder the packs already land in. That is on the connector list for this council."

"Is it judging management": "No. It shows what they wrote, next to what they wrote before. The judgment is Matt's. Every output is a draft he approves."

"What about the numbers": "The financial dashboard is Scott's Fabric work. This reads the words. Matt separated the two himself."

"Can I ask it anything": "In the room it answers six questions. With a key it reads the ledger live, and the code checks every sentence it returns against the reports before it is shown. It cannot show a sentence management did not write."

## Presenter controls
- **Shift+P**: the presenter menu. Reset to August; Jump to state (july, august, august-approved, monday); "[Month] reports arrive" runs the same reading log as the buttons on screen; Dictate a note.
- **Time Machine**: Go back in time in the header, or from the amber band. Up and Down (or Left and Right) step a month; Home is January, End is August; Escape keeps the month in view; Return to August exits.
- **Amber band**: while a past month is in view. Add [next month] reports, Go back in time, Return to August.

## If time is short
Cut Beat 6 first, then Beat 9. If very short, load `august`, open Harlan, and start at Beat 4. Never skip the stack or Beat 3.

## What not to say
- No real company names, even as examples. Say "a portfolio company."
- No claim that it reads Greenbriar's SharePoint today. Say "the folder the packs land in."
- No accuracy percentage. Say "every quote links to its page."
- No hours-saved number until Matt gives one. Say "Sunday night" and stop.
- Do not call the flags a score. They are flags.
- Do not say the tool "decided" anything. It read, it flagged, it drafted. Matt approved.
