# Pellston Intermodal

## Monthly report to the board, February 2026

Prepared by Anita Desai, CEO. Distributed to the board on March 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $10.9M | $11.0M | -0.1 |
| EBITDA | $1.4M | $1.4M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 12.6% | 12.5% | +0.1 pts |

Revenue for the month was $10.9M, 1.0% below budget. EBITDA was $1.4M, -0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Chassis availability improved during the month at all three ramps. Dwell time at the Kansas City ramp stayed under two days.

Looking ahead, peak season bookings are tracking ahead of last year.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Yard management system

Board target: live at all three ramps by Q3 2026. Owner: Anita Desai.

Yard management hardware was installed at the Kansas City ramp in February. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

### Drayage fleet renewal

Board target: 40 new tractors in service by Q4 2026. Owner: Carl Whitmore, VP Operations.

The first ten drayage tractors were delivered in February. No board action is requested on this item.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 423 at month end.
