# Redfern Distribution Partners

## Monthly report to the board, July 2026

Prepared by Helen Marchetti, CEO. Distributed to the board on August 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $14.3M | $14.5M | -0.2 |
| EBITDA | $1.3M | $1.3M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 8.8% | 9.0% | -0.2 pts |

Revenue for the month was $14.3M, 1.2% below budget. EBITDA was $1.3M, -0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Order volumes were in line with plan across the branch network. Fill rates held above the service target for the month.

Looking ahead, the fall promotional calendar supports the second half plan.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Columbus distribution center expansion

Board target: expansion open by Q4 2026. Owner: Helen Marchetti.

Interior fit-out of the Columbus expansion is underway. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.

### Online order share to 30%

Board target: 30% of orders online by Q4 2026. Owner: Rosa Delgado, VP Commercial.

Online orders were 26% of volume in July. The steering group met during the month.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 522 at month end.
