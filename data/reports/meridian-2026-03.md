## CEO commentary

The operating team held its monthly safety review with all terminal managers.

The maintenance shop cleared its backlog of scheduled tractor services.

Volume was in line with plan and the network ran without major disruption.

Looking ahead, diesel prices are the main variable in the quarterly outlook.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Pricing initiative, +2 to 3 pts by Q3

Board target: 2 to 3 points of net price by Q3 2026. Owner: Luis Ortega.

East region pricing went live in March. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

### Driver retention program

Board target: turnover under 40% by year end. Owner: Renee Calloway, VP Operations.

Driver turnover at 46%. The revised home-time schedule is live at all terminals. No board action is requested on this item.

### Warehouse automation pilot, Memphis

Board target: pilot results by Q4 2026. Owner: Renee Calloway, VP Operations.
