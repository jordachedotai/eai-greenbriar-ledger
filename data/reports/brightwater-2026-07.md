## CEO commentary

Cold storage occupancy at Greenville held above 90% through the month. The transport fleet ran at plan with on-time delivery in the mid nineties.

Looking ahead, produce season should keep occupancy high into the fall.

## Strategic initiatives

### Trailer temperature monitoring

Board target: full fleet monitored by Q2 2026. Owner: Devon Ashby.

Temperature exception reports covered the full fleet in July. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

### Greenville cold storage expansion

Board target: 40,000 pallet positions open by Q4 2026. Owner: Tessa Holloway, VP Facilities.

Refrigeration equipment for the Greenville expansion was ordered in July. No board action is requested on this item.
