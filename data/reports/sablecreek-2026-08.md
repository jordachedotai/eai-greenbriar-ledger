## CEO commentary

Looking ahead, refinery maintenance schedules support fleet utilization into the fourth quarter.

## Strategic initiatives

Board target: telematics across the rental fleet by Q2 2026. Owner: Felix Moreau.

Telematics data is now part of the weekly rental utilization review. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.

### Safety program, recordable rate under 1.0

Board target: recordable rate under 1.0 by Q4 2026. Owner: Gloria Pham, VP Safety.

The recordable incident rate held at 1.1 in August, on plan for under 1.0 by Q4. The steering group met during the month.
