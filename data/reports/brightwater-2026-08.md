## CEO commentary

The transport fleet ran at plan with on-time delivery in the mid nineties. Energy cost per pallet position was flat on the prior month.

Looking ahead, produce season should keep occupancy high into the fall.

The Greenville expansion remains on plan for Q4. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.
