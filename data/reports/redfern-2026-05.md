# Redfern Distribution Partners

## Monthly report to the board, May 2026

Prepared by Helen Marchetti, CEO. Distributed to the board on June 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $14.6M | $14.5M | +0.1 |
| EBITDA | $1.3M | $1.3M | +0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 9.2% | 9.0% | +0.2 pts |

Revenue for the month was $14.6M, 0.8% above budget. EBITDA was $1.3M, +0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Fill rates held above the service target for the month. Freight cost as a share of revenue was flat on the prior month.

Looking ahead, the fall promotional calendar supports the second half plan.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Columbus distribution center expansion

Board target: expansion open by Q4 2026. Owner: Helen Marchetti.

The Columbus expansion building was enclosed in May. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

### Online order share to 30%

Board target: 30% of orders online by Q4 2026. Owner: Rosa Delgado, VP Commercial.

Online orders were 24% of volume in May. No board action is requested on this item.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 519 at month end.
