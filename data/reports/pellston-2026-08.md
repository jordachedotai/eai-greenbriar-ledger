# Pellston Intermodal

## Monthly report to the board, August 2026

Prepared by Anita Desai, CEO. Distributed to the board on September 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $11.1M | $11.0M | +0.1 |
| EBITDA | $1.4M | $1.4M | +0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 12.4% | 12.5% | -0.1 pts |

Revenue for the month was $11.1M, 1.2% above budget. EBITDA was $1.4M, +0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Chassis availability improved during the month at all three ramps. Dwell time at the Kansas City ramp stayed under two days.

Looking ahead, peak season bookings are tracking ahead of last year.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Yard management system

Board target: live at all three ramps by Q3 2026. Owner: Anita Desai.

The Memphis ramp remains on plan for a September go-live. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.

### Drayage fleet renewal

Board target: 40 new tractors in service by Q4 2026. Owner: Carl Whitmore, VP Operations.

Drayage fleet renewal remains on plan with thirty in service and ten due in Q4. The steering group met during the month.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 435 at month end.
