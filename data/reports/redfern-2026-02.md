# Redfern Distribution Partners

## Monthly report to the board, February 2026

Prepared by Helen Marchetti, CEO. Distributed to the board on March 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $14.6M | $14.5M | +0.1 |
| EBITDA | $1.3M | $1.3M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 8.8% | 9.0% | -0.2 pts |

Revenue for the month was $14.6M, 1.0% above budget. EBITDA was $1.3M, -0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Fill rates held above the service target for the month. Freight cost as a share of revenue was flat on the prior month.

Looking ahead, the fall promotional calendar supports the second half plan.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Columbus distribution center expansion

Board target: expansion open by Q4 2026. Owner: Helen Marchetti.

Permits for the Columbus expansion were issued in February. No board action is requested on this item.

### Online order share to 30%

Board target: 30% of orders online by Q4 2026. Owner: Rosa Delgado, VP Commercial.

Online orders reached 22% of volume in February. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 513 at month end.
