## CEO commentary

Fuel surcharge recovery kept pace with diesel prices during the month.

Customer retention remained strong, with no top twenty accounts lost.

The operating team held its monthly safety review with all terminal managers.

Looking ahead, volume should follow the normal seasonal pattern into the summer.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Pricing initiative, +2 to 3 pts by Q3

Board target: 2 to 3 points of net price by Q3 2026. Owner: Luis Ortega.

The Central region pricing rollout is underway. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.

### Driver retention program

Board target: turnover under 40% by year end. Owner: Renee Calloway, VP Operations.

Driver turnover at 44%. The program remains on plan for under 40% by year end. The steering group met during the month.

### Warehouse automation pilot, Memphis

Board target: pilot results by Q4 2026. Owner: Renee Calloway, VP Operations.
