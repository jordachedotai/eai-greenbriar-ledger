## CEO commentary

The Gulf terminal completed its annual equipment inspection.

Trailer utilization improved slightly with the seasonal pattern.

Cash collections were ahead of plan and days sales outstanding were flat.

Less than truckload density improved on the eastern lanes.

Looking ahead, we expect the eastern lanes to carry most of the growth.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Pricing initiative, +2 to 3 pts by Q3

Board target: 2 to 3 points of net price by Q3 2026. Owner: Luis Ortega.

The regional pricing rollout begins in the East region in March. The steering group met during the month.

### Driver retention program

Board target: turnover under 40% by year end. Owner: Renee Calloway, VP Operations.

Driver turnover at 47%. Referral bonuses launched in February. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

### Warehouse automation pilot, Memphis

Board target: pilot results by Q4 2026. Owner: Renee Calloway, VP Operations.

<!-- page 3 -->
Pilot equipment for Memphis was ordered in February. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.
