# Pellston Intermodal

## Monthly report to the board, June 2026

Prepared by Anita Desai, CEO. Distributed to the board on July 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $11.1M | $11.0M | +0.1 |
| EBITDA | $1.4M | $1.4M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 12.3% | 12.5% | -0.2 pts |

Revenue for the month was $11.1M, 0.5% above budget. EBITDA was $1.4M, -0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Dwell time at the Kansas City ramp stayed under two days. Rail volumes through the three ramps were in line with plan.

Looking ahead, peak season bookings are tracking ahead of last year.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Yard management system

Board target: live at all three ramps by Q3 2026. Owner: Anita Desai.

The Dallas ramp went live on the yard management system in June. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

### Drayage fleet renewal

Board target: 40 new tractors in service by Q4 2026. Owner: Carl Whitmore, VP Operations.

The final ten drayage tractors are scheduled for delivery in Q4. No board action is requested on this item.

## People
