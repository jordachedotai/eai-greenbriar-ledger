# Brightwater Cold Chain

## Monthly report to the board, February 2026

Prepared by Devon Ashby, CEO. Distributed to the board on March 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $6.0M | $6.0M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA | $1.1M | $1.1M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 17.9% | 18.0% | -0.1 pts |

Revenue for the month was $6.0M, 0.2% below budget. EBITDA was $1.1M, -0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

The transport fleet ran at plan with on-time delivery in the mid nineties. Energy cost per pallet position was flat on the prior month.

Looking ahead, produce season should keep occupancy high into the fall.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Trailer temperature monitoring

Board target: full fleet monitored by Q2 2026. Owner: Devon Ashby.

Monitoring units were fitted to 120 trailers in February. The steering group met during the month.

### Greenville cold storage expansion

Board target: 40,000 pallet positions open by Q4 2026. Owner: Tessa Holloway, VP Facilities.

Design of the Greenville expansion was completed in February. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 393 at month end.
