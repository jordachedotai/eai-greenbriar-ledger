## Monthly report to the board, April 2026

Prepared by Ruth Adeyemi, CEO. Distributed to the board on May 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $5.4M | $5.5M | -0.1 |

Looking ahead, the industrial customer base supports the second half plan.
