# Brightwater Cold Chain

## Monthly report to the board, March 2026

Prepared by Devon Ashby, CEO. Distributed to the board on April 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $5.9M | $6.0M | -0.1 |
| EBITDA | $1.1M | $1.1M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 18.2% | 18.0% | +0.2 pts |

Revenue for the month was $5.9M, 1.2% below budget. EBITDA was $1.1M, -0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Energy cost per pallet position was flat on the prior month. Cold storage occupancy at Greenville held above 90% through the month.

Looking ahead, produce season should keep occupancy high into the fall.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Trailer temperature monitoring

Board target: full fleet monitored by Q2 2026. Owner: Devon Ashby.

Half of the trailer fleet now carries temperature monitoring. Weekly status reporting to the executive team continues.

### Greenville cold storage expansion

Board target: 40,000 pallet positions open by Q4 2026. Owner: Tessa Holloway, VP Facilities.

The general contractor for the Greenville expansion was appointed in March. No board action is requested on this item.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 396 at month end.
