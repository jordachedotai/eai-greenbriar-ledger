# Pellston Intermodal

## Monthly report to the board, April 2026

Prepared by Anita Desai, CEO. Distributed to the board on May 5, 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts.

## Financial summary

| Metric | Month actual | Month budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $11.0M | $11.0M | -0.0 |
| EBITDA | $1.4M | $1.4M | +0.0 |
| EBITDA margin | 12.7% | 12.5% | +0.2 pts |

Revenue for the month was $11.0M, 0.2% below budget. EBITDA was $1.4M, +0.0 to budget. Liquidity is within plan and all covenants were met at month end.

## CEO commentary

Rail volumes through the three ramps were in line with plan. Chassis availability improved during the month at all three ramps.

Looking ahead, peak season bookings are tracking ahead of last year.

## Strategic initiatives

Update on the initiatives agreed with the board in January, in board order.

### Yard management system

Board target: live at all three ramps by Q3 2026. Owner: Anita Desai.

The yard management rollout to the other two ramps remains on plan. Progress is reviewed at the monthly operating meeting.

### Drayage fleet renewal

Board target: 40 new tractors in service by Q4 2026. Owner: Carl Whitmore, VP Operations.

Drayage tractor deliveries continued in April at the planned pace. The steering group met during the month.

## People

No changes to the leadership team this month. Total headcount was 426 at month end.
